# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone. We do not tolerate harassment in any form.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior:**
- Be respectful and constructive
- Accept feedback gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the community

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, personal attacks, or trolling
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening a GitHub issue or contacting the maintainer. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
